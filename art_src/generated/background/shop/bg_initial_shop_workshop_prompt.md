# Initial Shop Workshop Background Prompt

用途: final
生成日期: 2026-05-31
负责人: Codex
是否进入正式素材: yes

## Design Intent

Create a single 1920x1080 initial shop background that visually connects to the new birth-point laboratory reference. The room should read as an adjacent safe repair workshop: central warm workbench, moderate weapon wall, right-side elevator/access-gate language, and wide foreground space for later player, NPC, and dialogue UI overlays.

## Prompt

Create a 1920x1080, 16:9 landscape pixel art background for a cyberpunk side-scrolling action game.

Use the attached birth lab reference image as the main style and spatial reference. The new scene should feel like an adjacent initial shop / repair room next to that birth lab, belonging to the same underground facility. Match the reference language: dark blue-black industrial metal walls, segmented wall panels, embedded pipes, service hatches, ceiling machinery, broad playable metal floor, cyan neon guide lights, tiny magenta status lights, and a few amber door-status lights.

Scene: an initial safe repair shop beside the birth point. The main visual focus is a central workbench under warm amber task lamps. On the workbench: fictional sci-fi gun parts, energy batteries, repair tools, welding gear, a small diagnostic terminal, parts bins, cables, and mechanical components. The workbench should feel slightly warmer and more lived-in than the cold birth lab, but still dark, industrial, and cyberpunk.

Behind the workbench, include a medium-sized weapon pegboard or rack on the back wall with a modest number of fictional sci-fi firearm silhouettes and modular parts: a pistol silhouette, compact gun, rifle silhouette, energy weapon outline, and attachments. The weapon wall must be readable but not dominate the entire scene. All weapons must be original futuristic designs, no real-world brands, no recognizable real gun models.

On the right side or rear background, include an elevator door, access gate, or lift-platform structure visually related to the right-side elevator in the birth lab reference: heavy dark metal, vertical cyan light strips, small amber status lights, caution stripe details. It should suggest the shop connects to the birth lab, without copying the reference exactly.

Foreground: keep a wide, clean, playable metal floor with clear negative space for a player character, shop NPC, and dialogue UI to be overlaid later. Do not draw the NPC. Leave an obvious interaction space in front of the workbench or counter. Medium detail density only: toolboxes, hanging cables, a small mechanical arm, maintenance screens, wall weapon rack, parts crates, scanline glow effects. The composition must stay clear: entrance/connection, workbench, weapon wall, elevator/access gate, and walkable foreground should all be easy to read.

Mood: cyberpunk machine-crisis, underground experimental facility, adjacent repair room, initial shop, safe supply point. Cold blue metal space with a small amount of warm amber task lighting. Crisp high-quality pixel art, sharp silhouettes, clean pixel clusters, high-contrast edges, dark industrial sci-fi texture, cyan holographic light, subtle glitch/scanline glow.

Strict constraints: no UI frames, no shop menu buttons, no readable signs, no text, no logo, no watermark, no human characters, no NPC visible, no medical bed as the main subject, no clone pods as the main subject, no bright white walls, no natural plants, no photorealism, no 3D render, no oil painting, no soft blur, no low-resolution output, no clutter that makes the scene unreadable, no real firearm brands or real firearm models.
